## What's fixed

- **QR codes scan reliably in AmneziaWG on Android.** QR codes in the panel (client connection, app install, two-factor setup) and on a client's personal page are now drawn as a sharp picture where every square has the same whole number of screen pixels. Before, squares came out of different sizes with blurred edges, and the AmneziaWG scanner on Android often failed to read the code or needed many tries. In the dark theme the white margin around a QR code is wider, because the phone camera overexposes a thin one, but it only takes free space: the code itself keeps its size. Thanks to @koo-pub.

## Update

**System → Update** in the panel (since 1.4.0), or on the server:

    awg-router update

New install (Ubuntu or Debian, as root):

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/r-slashslash/AWG-Router/main/install.sh | bash

## 修复内容

- **AmneziaWG（Android）可以可靠地扫描二维码。** 面板中（客户端连接、安装应用、设置双重验证）和客户端个人页面上的二维码现在以清晰的图片绘制，每个方块占用相同的整数个屏幕像素。以前方块大小不一、边缘模糊，Android 上的 AmneziaWG 扫描器经常无法识别，或需要多次尝试。在深色主题中，二维码周围的白边更宽，因为手机摄像头会使较窄的白边过曝，但白边只占用空余空间：二维码本身的尺寸不变。感谢 @koo-pub。
