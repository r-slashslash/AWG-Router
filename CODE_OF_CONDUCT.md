# Code of conduct

AWG Router is a small project, and people use it from many countries and in many
languages. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests and discussions) is
expected to keep it a friendly and useful place.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with questions and with language differences. Not everyone writes in their first language.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good intent, and explain yourself if you were misunderstood.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment and discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information, including server addresses, keys and
  panel links that are not yours.
- Spam, advertising and off-topic content.
- Asking for help to harm or spy on other people.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people
who break these rules. To report a problem, write privately through the
[Security tab](https://github.com/r-slashslash/AWG-Router/security/advisories/new) of the
repository and say it concerns conduct.

This text is a short adaptation of the ideas in the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
